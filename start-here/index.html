The simple version

What a bot is

A bot, around here, is just a helper you talk to in a chat window.

You tell it what's going on — the pets, the shop, the pile of sticky notes — and it drafts the boring bits so you're not staring at a blank screen.

It doesn't send, post, or pay for anything unless you say so. That's the whole deal.

Quick answers

FAQ

How much does this cost?

The starter guide is free. On Gumroad it shows up as $0+ — pay what you want, and $0 is fine. The full guides are $12, $14, or $15. There are a few bundles if you want two or four together. You'll also need a free Grok Bot account or the free ChatGPT app to actually use them.

What about my privacy?

These helpers are set up to draft only. They shouldn't send, post, buy, book, pay, or delete anything without your OK, and they shouldn't ask for your passwords. You decide what you paste in. Don't paste bank logins, Social Security numbers, or card numbers into a chat. If the bot isn't sure, it should ask you instead of guessing.

Do I need to be techy?

Nope. If you can open a link and press a button, you can do this. The full guides take about 15 minutes. If a step feels weird, skip it and come back — the bot works for you, not the other way around.
