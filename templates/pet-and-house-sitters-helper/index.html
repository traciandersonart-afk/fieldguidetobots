Pet & House Sitter's Helper

A daily “How is my pup doing?” update, a house calendar and checklist, and a calm care plan for senior or sick pets. The bot drafts; you send every update. Not veterinary advice.

Copy this templateGet the guide · $12

This is a planning and communication helper. It is not veterinary advice. Medicines, doses, and what counts as an emergency come from the pet’s vet and owner, in writing.

What it does

  • Turns an owner intake form and Pet Personality Profile into a whole-stay house calendar, a daily checklist, and a “Meet [pet]” card.
  • Drafts a short, warm “How is my pup doing?” update from your notes each evening, for you to send.
  • Runs stay reminders you turn on: morning checklist, meds, house tasks, an optional midday check, vet-appointment heads-up, and an end-of-stay report.
  • Builds a calm care plan and dose log from the owner’s and vet’s written notes for a senior or sick pet. It never guesses a dose.
  • Drafts a backup-sitter handoff and answers “who do I call?” from your Who to Call sheet only.
